# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Delegated to the implementation team. Expo and React Native share the mobile experience. ActivityKit and Kotlin supply native live flight surfaces.

## Users

Assumed from the brief: travelers checking imminent flights and people following a flight for a pickup.

## Product Purpose

Plano keeps live flight changes, upcoming trips, inbound aircraft, and trip history in one app.

## Operating Context

Assumed from the brief: people use Plano in short sessions with spotty connectivity, often while moving through an airport.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Flight tracking, alerts, and advanced tracking tools are available to everyone. Reported status, estimates, and stale observations are distinguished in the interface.
